# NOTES — the verses a hand or a ruling touched

*English. The companion to [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md): where that file tells how the whole was made,
this one names the verses where the text was written by hand, ruled, or left open — so a reader auditing
any verse can see whether it was machine-rendered, hand-rendered, or ruled, and why.*

---

## Rendered by hand

- **162 verses** carry `"model": "opus-4.8-hand"` — the hard tail of July 2026 (2026-07-24): verses
  where the machine scattered the marker, invented content, or leaked a register. Each was read from the
  Hebrew and rendered whole; the file says so.
- **103 aleph-tav homograph verses** (2026-07-24) resolved from the Hebrew by hand — whether a given את
  is the particle, the preposition, or part of another word.
- **Four sign words** (Exod 4:8 twice, 8:19, 12:13): הָאֹת / לְאֹת is the noun 'sign' (H226), not the
  particle; the glyph was taken off.
- **36 verses** of the 2026-10-07 marker repair, each cured by name against its Hebrew and rows —
  among them Num 20:28 'and Aaron died' (the subject, not an object); Gen 20:16 'I have given a thousand
  silver' (no את in the Hebrew there); Gen 35:3 'the El who answered ⟨את⟩ me'; Jer 41:9 'the pit';
  Num 30:9 'her husband disallows ⟨את⟩ her'; Josh 21:34 'from the tribe of Zebulun'; Josh 7:15 '⟨את⟩ him
  and ⟨את⟩ all that he has' (the row is אֹתוֹ).
- **Markers before a clause are lawful** and were kept: where את governs a whole clause, the glyph
  stands before it — '⟨את⟩ how I have walked' (2 Kgs 20:3, Isa 38:3), '⟨את⟩ which they raise up'
  (Lev 22:15), '⟨את⟩ who made for us' (Jer 38:16); also 1 Sam 2:22, Exod 10:2, Gen 30:29, Lev 5:16.

## Ruled

- **The Name** (2026-07-22): YHWH in every place; 'Lord GOD' → 'Lord YHWH' in 92 verses where an
  English convention had leaked in.
- **The Aramaic register** (2026-07-23): Elah across the Aramaic of Daniel and Ezra; Mare at Dan 2:47
  and 5:23.
- **Every את is shown** (2026-07-22) — the object marker and the preposition 'with' alike, the
  from-compound מֵאֵת included (Lev 25:44, Jer 26:1, 1 Kgs 20:25).
- **Where the glyph stands beside 'with'** (2026-10-07): after the preposition, before its object —
  'with ⟨את⟩ him', 'from ⟨את⟩ Hanameel'.
- **1 Sam 17:4, 17:23** (2026-10-04): אִישׁ הַבֵּנַיִם, 'the-man-of-the-between-two' — the dual
  kept audible (it had read 'a man of the ⟨duelists⟩' and 'the man ⟨of⟩ the duel').

## Open

- *(closed 2026-10-07)* The 122 verses the restore could not place mechanically: 94 placed by the
  nearest occurrence to the Hebrew word's place, every one read (two wrong picks corrected by hand —
  2 Chr 22:6 'when he fought with ⟨את⟩ Hazael', Exod 29:28 'offering from ⟨את⟩ the children of Israel');
  28 written by hand against the Hebrew — among them Isa 53:9 'And he gave with ⟨את⟩ the wicked his grave,
  and ⟨את⟩ the rich in his death'; Ruth 2:20 'his kindness with ⟨את⟩ the living and with ⟨את⟩ the dead';
  Jer 23:28 'What is the straw to ⟨את⟩ the grain?'; Zech 14:17 'whoever does not go up from ⟨את⟩ the
  families of the earth'. Older faults met there, cured: Gen 24:40 'with thee … thy way … thou shalt'
  (the rows say you, your); Gen 47:22 a stray 'with,'; 2 Sam 3:12 two quotations bracketed as if
  supplied; Exod 34:27 the row 'and and with Israel'.
- *(closed 2026-10-07)* **'With' written in** where the glyph had stood for it and the English never
  wrote the word — the row is the preposition: 1 Sam 23:23 'I will go with ⟨את⟩ you'; 2 Sam 3:20 'and with
  ⟨את⟩ him ⟨were⟩ twenty men'; 2 Sam 15:11 'And with ⟨את⟩ Absalom went two hundred men'; Lev 16:16 'that
  dwells with ⟨את⟩ them'; Gen 42:7 'spoke with ⟨את⟩ them harsh things'; Gen 43:16 'saw with ⟨את⟩ them
  ⟨את⟩ Benjamin'.
- **A glyph inside a bracket — 7 verses**, from before the repair: Exod 1:16, 11:1, Deut 11:27,
  Judg 3:1, 18:24, 2 Sam 14:29, Ezek 17:17 ('⟨⟨את⟩ them⟩'). To be parted.
- **OSHB and TAHOT disagree** in 45 verses on whether an את is the object marker or 'with'. Both are
  kept as lenses.
