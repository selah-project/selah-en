# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Selah open-corpus community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, traditions, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them; and
- focusing on what serves the text, its readers, and the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

Disagreement about language, interpretation, theology, or method is not itself
misconduct. Address the evidence and the reading without attacking the person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that conflict with these standards. They may issue a correction,
warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban in proportion to the impact
and pattern of conduct.

Report abusive or harassing behavior privately to
`support@selahproject.com` with **Code of conduct** in the subject. Reports will
be reviewed fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be
respected as far as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public.
